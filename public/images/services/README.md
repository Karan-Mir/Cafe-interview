# Service-card artwork

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. WebP deliverables are 768 × 512 and are precached for offline use.

Shared prompt: Professional editorial 3D still life for a cafe service card. Sage green, forest green, warm ivory, restrained brass accents, matte paper and ceramic materials, soft studio daylight, realistic contact shadows, landscape 3:2 composition. Objects fully visible; no text, letters, numbers, logos or watermark.

Subjects by filename:

- report: monthly paper report with three charts and checkmarks beside a coffee cup.
- alert: coffee cup, bean pouch and smartphone with a price-rise notification.
- menu: illustrated cafe menu and calculator.
- calendar: weekly calendar highlighting busy time blocks with coffee cups.
- stock: supply crate with coffee beans, milk and inventory clipboard.
- waste: smartphone photographing a bin of pastry leftovers and coffee grounds.
- invoice: smartphone scanning a purchase receipt beside coffee beans.
- social: smartphone showing a cappuccino-and-pastry social post.
- chat: smartphone chat bubbles with a reservation card and coffee cup.
- compare: three equally sized miniature cafe storefronts beside a comparison bar chart.
- loyalty: smartphone customer cards and a return arrow beside coffee.
- clock: desk clock beside a cost-versus-sales chart and coffee cup.
- checklist: staff clipboard with checkmarks, cleaning cloth, thermometer and portafilter.
- assistant: smartphone conversation bubbles showing tea and coffee, beside a sales chart.

The comparison image used an editorial product-photography variation of the shared prompt. The source PNGs remain in the generation output directory; these compressed images are the production deliverables.
